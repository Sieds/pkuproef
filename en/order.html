How to order the PKU cookbook?

The first cookbook 'PKU Proef: PKU PROOF: 101 delicious low-protein recipes' is a full-colour edition with 101 recipes and 51 eye-catching photos. The book has 189 pages in a soft cover, but is now sold out.

The second cookbook 'PKU Proef: Doorproeven' is still available and makes use of even more low-protein products that have become available in supermarkets, such as cassava, jackfruit and konjac root. This too is a full-colour edition, with 80 recipes that are all accompanied by a mouth-watering photo. The book also has 189 pages and is bound in a soft cover.

A cookbook costs € 24.95, excluding shipping. Our cookbooks just fit through the letterbox. Therefore, the packaging and shipping costs within the Netherlands are € 5.00. For shipping to countries outside the Netherlands, the shipping costs are higher, depending on the country from € 12.00.

You can order the PKU cookbook by sending an email to info@pkuproef.nl. In the subject of the mail, enter 'Order PKU cookbook: Doorproeven' and tell us in the mail which country you live in so we can inform you of the correct shipping costs. You will then receive a reply email with the order procedure. After payment, you will receive the cookbook within 10 working days in your letterbox.
